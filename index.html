Changheon Han

Institute for Physical Artificial Intelligence (IPAI) Postdoctoral Fellow

School of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Purdue University · han711@purdue.edu · linkedin.com/in/changheon

I am an Institute for Physical Artificial Intelligence (IPAI) Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of Aeronautics and Astronautics at Purdue University, where I work on physical AI for human–autonomy teaming and robotic In-Space Servicing, Assembly, and Manufacturing (ISAM), mentored by Prof. Shaoshuai Mou. My research develops generalizable and human-centered physical AI that connects human intent with engineering reasoning, physical prediction, and the orchestration of manufacturing resources, both on Earth and, increasingly, in space.

I earned my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at Purdue University (2022–2026, advisor: Prof. Martin B.G. Jun), with a dissertation on generalizable and edge-deployable industrial AI via foundation models, lightweight models, and generative multimodal monitoring. Before my Ph.D., I spent seven years (2015–2021) as a researcher at the Korea Aerospace Research Institute (KARI), where I worked on the KSLV-II launch vehicle propulsion system, lunar and satellite programs, and the KF-21 fighter jet, and served as PI on a mixed-reality spacecraft design project with Microsoft. I also served as an AI Research Fellow at Poseidon (2025–2026), working on deepfake detection and speech models. I hold an M.S. and B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Sogang University.

My research interests span three connected areas: generalizable and human-centered physical AI for manufacturing automation; human-robot collaboration and multi-robot systems for in-space servicing, assembly, and manufacturing; and autonomous in-situ resource utilization systems for planetary manufacturing. Details on my current and future research directions are on the Research page.
